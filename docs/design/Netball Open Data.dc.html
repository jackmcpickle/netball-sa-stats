Method & data

A club's championship score for a season is the sum, across every grade it fields a team in, of its ladder position converted to points — a team finishing first in a grade of ten earns ten, second earns nine, and so on — with each grade multiplied by a weight reflecting its standard.

Weights run from 1.0 for Premier Division down to 0.4 for the lower metro and city night divisions. This rewards clubs for depth as well as for a strong top team: a club fielding eight sides that all finish mid-table can outscore a club with one premiership-winning team and nothing else.

Ladders are computed from match results, not entered directly. Points are two for a win and one for a draw; teams level on points are separated by goal percentage (goals for ÷ goals against × 100), the standard Netball SA method.

This is sample data

Every result on this site is synthetic, generated to demonstrate the structure. Club names are real South Australian clubs; the scores, ladders and rankings are not. Wire in PlayHQ or Netball SA exports to make it real.
